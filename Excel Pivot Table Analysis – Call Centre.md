# Excel Pivot Table Analysis – Call Centre Performance

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing call centre operations and sales performance using Microsoft Excel Pivot Tables. The objective is to transform raw call centre data into meaningful business insights that help management understand customer behaviour, sales trends, employee performance, and operational efficiency.

The analysis uses Pivot Tables, calculated fields, filters, slicers, conditional formatting, and Pivot Charts to answer important business questions and support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze call volume and customer engagement.
- Identify top-performing customers based on purchase value.
- Evaluate customer satisfaction levels.
- Understand the relationship between call duration and purchase amount.
- Identify peak business periods and busiest days.
- Monitor year-to-date (YTD) sales performance.
- Evaluate employee performance and identify training opportunities.
- Assess staffing requirements during high-volume periods.

## Key Business Questions

The project answers the following business questions:

1. How many calls are we receiving from each customer?
2. How satisfied are our customers?
3. Who are our top 10 customers?
4. Who are the top 10 customers for a specific representative?
5. What is the relationship between call duration and purchase amount?
6. How busy is the call centre throughout the year?
7. What is the year-to-date (YTD) sales performance?
8. Which days of the week are the busiest?
9. Is there a relationship between call duration and customer satisfaction?
10. Should we hire additional employees during specific months?
11. Which customers spend significant time calling but generate comparatively less revenue?
12. Which representatives could benefit from customer satisfaction training?

## Key Insights & Findings

- **Total Calls:** Analyzed 1,000 call records.
- **Total Purchase Amount:** 96,623.
- **Call Duration:** Calls lasting 1–2 hours contributed approximately 52.68% of total purchase amount.
- **Customer Satisfaction:** Overall average satisfaction rating was approximately 3.89 out of 5.
- **Peak Month:** March recorded the highest call volume, with 155 calls.
- **Busiest Day:** Saturday recorded the highest overall share of calls.
- **Sales Performance:** Cumulative YTD sales reached 96,623 by December.
- **Employee Training:** All five representatives showed average satisfaction ratings between approximately 3.85 and 3.95, indicating an opportunity for a common customer service training programme.
- **Staffing Recommendation:** Temporary staffing, flexible scheduling, and workload redistribution can be considered during high-volume months such as March, April, and October.
